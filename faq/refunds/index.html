Standard refunds

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the digital item has not been consumed. Refunds are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method.

Partial refunds

After 14 days, partial refunds may be offered at agent discretion for service outages lasting more than 24 hours.

How to request

Open a ticket from the in-app Help section and choose category Billing > Refund. Include the order ID shown on the receipt.
